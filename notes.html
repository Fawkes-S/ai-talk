Finally, what is the environmental cost?

AI runs in data centres. These need electricity, and some use water for cooling. If the power comes from fossil fuels, it also creates pollution.

数据｜用电估算与预测

The International Energy Agency expects data centre electricity use to roughly double from twenty twenty-five to twenty thirty. This includes centres used for AI and other services.

新数据｜本地民意

A twenty twenty-six Gallup survey found that about seven in ten Americans opposed AI data centres in their own area. Concerns included electricity costs and the environment.

So AI may help one person while creating costs for someone else. As it develops, we need to ask who benefits and who pays.
